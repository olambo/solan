## Almost

Earth's gravity is almost strong enough to trap chemical rockets on the surface forever. At a planet ten percent more massive, hydrocarbons don't get you out. Distance is almost too far — 4.2 light years is close enough to be conceivable, far enough to require 600 years and a ship that is mostly fuel. Interstellar dust is almost too destructive — survivable at 0.02c, lethal at 0.2c. Time is almost too long for any programme built around human lifespans.

Almost. In every direction, the margin is real but thin.

The Voidfarer does not make interstellar travel look easy. It makes it look barely possible — which is exactly the right tone. Every tonne of shielding, every year of transit, every kilogram of propellant is a direct response to a universe that offers no shortcuts and no encouragement.

This is not optimism. It is defiance.
